How to write the list

The simplest version is just your prompts, one per paragraph:

A close-up of coarse salt in a scarred hand.

A dark kitchen with brass vessels on a shelf.

Add characters once, then tag the prompts that use them. Add a STYLES block for wording you'd otherwise repeat in every prompt — it gets appended automatically:

SETTINGS
name: my-images
aspect ratio: 16:9

CHARACTERS
sudha: Sudha — a 43-year-old woman, greying hair…

STYLES
kitchen: Photorealistic, deep ochre, film grain, 8k.

PROMPTS
characters: sudha
style: kitchen
A close-up of coarse salt in a scarred hand.

opening-shot:
style: kitchen
A dark kitchen with brass vessels on a shelf.

Every section is optional. Name a prompt by starting its block with opening-shot:, otherwise they're numbered for you. If a prompt needs blank lines inside it, separate prompts with --- instead.
